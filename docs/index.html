    How it works, and your all-time trouble spots

    Every round shuffles all 28 numbered paragraphs (Freud 1–12, Lasch 1–16). A paragraph is cleared when you answer it correctly. If you miss it, it is reinserted a few cards later and must then be answered correctly twice in a row before it clears, like an Anki lapse. Paragraphs you have missed in earlier rounds are shuffled toward the front of the next round. Progress is saved in this browser.
